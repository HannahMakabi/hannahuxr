Audience Management & Segmentation

✓ Shipped

Research question: How do users actually organize contacts and audiences?

Methods

User InterviewsJTBDCompetitor AnalysisWorkflow Evaluation

Insight

Users don't think in tags/attributes. They think in real-world groups like VIP customers, event attendees, and repeat buyers.

Research contribution

Mapped how users naturally group and identify their audiences, uncovering gaps between their mental model and MailDrip's existing segmentation structure.

Impact:

  • Led in-depth tags & segmentation research.
  • Restructured segmentation around the user mental model, not system logic.

Email Deliverability & Spam

✓ Shipped

Research question: How do users understand deliverability and why emails land in spam?

Methods

Competitor AnalysisDeliverability ResearchUser Feedback Analysis

Insight

Users were worried about spam placement but had little guidance on content quality, spam triggers, or sender reputation.

Research contribution

Investigated the gap between what users believed affected deliverability and what they were actually doing that could impact email performance.

Impact:

  • Recommended spam-checking guidance, content quality indicators, and an education flow.
  • Implemented & Shipped.

AI Content Creation

✓ Shipped

Research question: How can AI support email creation and reduce setup effort?

Methods

User InterviewsCompetitor AnalysisConcept Evaluation

Insight

Users wanted AI assistance but were unclear about how to use it and what it could actually help them accomplish.

Research contribution

Explored users' expectations, concerns, and understanding of AI within the email creation workflow.

Impact:

  • Defined AI-assisted email generation, content guidance, and workflow support.
  • Led AI education research to make AI clearer and more trustworthy for creation. Shipped — AI Education Flow.

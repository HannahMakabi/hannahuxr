/ 03 About Me

Curiosity has always shaped how I see the world.
UX Research simply gave it a purpose.

I didn't start out as a UX Researcher.

I started as a designer creating screens and experiences. But the more I designed, the more curious I became about the people on the other side of those screens.

Why were users struggling with a feature that seemed obvious to us?

Why did some experiences work while others didn't?

Why were people behaving differently from what we expected?

I've always been someone who asks a lot of questions, and UX Research gave me a way to turn that curiosity into something useful.

Today, I help teams understand people through research so they can make better product decisions. My work spans discovery research, Jobs to Be Done studies, usability testing, behavioral analytics, onboarding research, competitor analysis, and product strategy. Whether I'm interviewing users, analyzing behaviour, or validating ideas, my goal is always the same: helping teams build products that solve real problems.

I enjoy working with cross functional teams to uncover user needs, challenge assumptions, and ensure product decisions are grounded in evidence rather than guesswork.

Outside of research, I care a lot about people. I volunteer with initiatives focused on women and young girls, especially around technology and climate education. Being part of those spaces has taught me that good decisions start with listening first, which is probably why I enjoy research so much in the first place.

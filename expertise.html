My Research Approach

01

Start with Evidence

I don't begin with assumptions. Every project starts by understanding the problem through research before discussing solutions.

02

Listen Beyond Words

The best insights often come from what users hesitate to say, not just what they tell us directly.

03

Connect the Dots

Patterns matter more than isolated comments. I turn research findings into clear insights that help teams move forward with confidence.

04

Validate Before, During & After Building

I validate ideas with users before building, then continue validating after launch to make sure the final experience solves the right problem. Research doesn't stop when something ships, real users can reveal new issues, gaps, and opportunities that prototypes cannot always predict.

05

Bring Teams Together

Research has the greatest impact when product, design, engineering, and stakeholders share the same understanding of users. I help teams align around evidence before building, during development, and after launch as we continue learning from real user experiences.
